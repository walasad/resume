Waleed Al-Asad

Data Scientist III, Forward Deployed at Mozn AI · Riyadh, Saudi Arabia

About

I am a data scientist with three years of client-facing work in banking, public finance, healthcare, education and Hajj operations, building models that run inside the client’s environment. I am the author of autoLRP, an open-source library for explaining PyTorch models.

Selected Work

In production at Mozn AI

Anti-fraud, Alrajhi Bank. Turned card-fraud rule discovery into a local-search optimization, taking a weeks-long manual process to minutes; the deployed rules cut card fraud by about 25%. Real-time mule-cashout controls prevent about 3M SAR/month at 50% precision.

Budget validation, Ministry of Finance (NCGR). Fine-tuned embedding models so comptrollers can cross-check allocations: 70% Hits@5, against 28% for off-the-shelf embeddings. Two-stage OCR for handwritten Arabic digits.

Digital Mentor, Health Specialties Commission (SCFHS). A Bayesian model (PyMC, MCMC) of rank-based nominations that simulates each candidate’s chance of nomination: F1 about 0.84, against 0.39 for the baseline. Bias testing and outcome KPIs endorsed by the Deputy Secretary.

Learner analytics, National eLearning Center (NeLC). Led 30+ analytical and ML models for 200,000+ learners, including course recommendation and a learning-pathway optimizer. API response time cut from about 5 s to 150 ms.

Season analytics, Ministry of Hajj and Umrah. Analyzed 100M+ records in Polars, finding fraudulent campaigns and price manipulation; the reports informed the Deputy Minister’s enforcement decisions.

Proofs of concept

Public-services assistant, MEWA. RAG assistant over 300+ services, served with vLLM on GCP; a custom tree-structured retriever scored 96% against 82% for an OpenAI-embedding baseline.

Medical exam question generator, SCFHS. Grounded LLM generator for SMLE-standard questions at 2.5× baseline throughput; won a second-phase engagement.

Compliance auditing, Central Bank (SAMA). Agentic LLM pipeline that turns policy documents into checks, runs them against an audited entity’s data and reports the gaps.

Open Source

autoLRP (author). Layer-wise Relevance Propagation for any PyTorch model without a rewrite, tested on CNNs, ViT, LLMs, Mamba and world models. MIT-licensed, on PyPI as autolrp.

needle. A PyTorch-like framework built from scratch: autodiff, a C++ backend, a layers API and a Transformer language model on top.

SimEngine. Text-similarity toolkit with BERT, FastText and NER-based methods.
